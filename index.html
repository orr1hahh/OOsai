About Oosai's Garden

What This Is

Oosai's Garden is a free, open, interconnected library of wisdom combining the I Ching, Western and Mayan astrology, world mythology, poetry, philosophy, and personal reflection.

It's not a tool. It's a world. A space to explore the relationships between symbols, stars, stories, dreams, and your own becoming.

The Core Rooms

  • Book of Changes: All 64 hexagrams with original essays, archetypes, mythological stories, and changing-line reflections.
  • Celestial Theatre: Birth chart calculations with Western astrology and Mayan Tzolk'in cosmology. See your planetary archetypes and life progressions.
  • Garden of Words: Curated collections of poetry, philosophy, and literature from world traditions. Create reading pathways. Save your reflections.
  • Dream Garden: Record dreams. Build a personal symbol dictionary. Discover patterns and connections.
  • Cosmic Connection: Create a soulful profile. Find kindred spirits. Explore compatibility.
  • Inner Library: Organize everything you save into collections. Export anytime.

Key Principles

  • Completely Free: No paywalls, no premium features, no ads, forever.
  • No Accounts Required to Read: Everything public is accessible without signing up.
  • Your Data Stays With You: Stored in your browser. No servers. No tracking. No selling.
  • Privacy First: Share only what you choose. Block anyone anytime.
  • Original vs. Classical: Oosai interpretations are marked as contemporary. We respect sources.
  • It's Your Garden: Export, backup, migrate your data anytime. It's yours.
