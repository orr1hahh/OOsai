The Garden Remembers

Original reflection by Oosai

In every garden, there is a memory keeper. Not a witness who judges, but a presence that holds the shape of what has grown here. The light that struck the leaf. The rain that fell on thirsty soil. The moment a seed broke open.

The garden does not forget. It transforms. The fallen flower becomes mulch becomes soil becomes root of the next growth. Nothing is wasted. Everything is remembered in its next form.

About Oosai's Garden

What This Is

Oosai's Garden is a free, interconnected library of wisdom combining the I Ching, Western and Mayan astrology, mythology, poetry, and personal reflection.

It's not a tool. It's a world. A space to explore the relationships between symbols, stars, stories, and your own becoming.

Key Features

  • Book of Changes: All 64 hexagrams with original essays, archetypes, mythological stories.
  • Celestial Theatre: Birth chart with Western astrology and Mayan Tzolk'in. See planetary archetypes, transits, life progressions.
  • Dream Garden: Record dreams, build symbol dictionary, discover unconscious patterns.
  • Garden of Words: Write freely. Save reflections. Discover reading pathways.
  • Cosmic Connection: Find kindred spirits. Explore synastry. Share interpretations.
  • Inner Library: Everything you save in one personal space. Export anytime.

Important Information

  • Completely Free: No paywalls, no ads.
  • No Accounts to Read: Everything public is accessible.
  • Your Data Stays With You: Stored in your browser only.
  • Privacy First: Share only what you choose.
  • Block Anyone: You control your connections.
